Contact us

Email barankaraoguz.business@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the StudyGlow version (shown on the App Store page). For subscription questions, your App Store order ID helps us find the purchase.

Restore your purchase

  1. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  2. Open StudyGlow and go to Settings, then tap Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore purchases at the top of the Premium screen.
  3. If an active subscription is found, Premium unlocks right away. Otherwise StudyGlow tells you that no active subscription was found for this Apple Account.

Manage or cancel your subscription

In StudyGlow, go to Settings › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › StudyGlow. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge; Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your sessions, subjects, goals and exams are stored only on your iPhone. StudyGlow has no account and no cloud sync.

  • Delete an exam: in Exams, swipe left on it, or open it and tap Delete exam.
  • Delete everything: delete the StudyGlow app from your iPhone. This removes all study sessions, exams and settings from the device and cannot be undone. Existing iCloud or computer backups of your device may still contain a copy until they are replaced.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; manage that in your Apple Account.

Usage analytics

StudyGlow can share anonymous usage statistics to help improve the app. Turn this on or off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Analytics. It never includes your subjects or exam names, and Premium works the same either way. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Frequently asked questions

Does the timer keep running if I leave the app?

Yes. StudyGlow keeps time from the moment you start, so the session continues while the app is in the background or closed. Turn on Notify me when time’s up before starting (or Timer completion alerts in Settings) to get an alert when it ends.

Why didn’t I get a notification when my session ended?

Check that timer alerts are on in StudyGlow and that notifications for StudyGlow are allowed in iOS Settings › Notifications. If you declined permission earlier, StudyGlow shows a shortcut to open iOS Settings.

Does paused time count?

No. Only focused time counts toward your session, daily goal and streak.

What happens if I end a session early?

If you focused for at least 1 minute, the time is saved and marked “Partial”. Ending in under a minute saves nothing.

How does the streak work?

A day counts toward your streak when one session reaches at least 10 focused minutes, including a partial session. Days follow your iPhone’s local calendar.

How do I change my daily goal?

Go to Settings › Daily goal and choose 30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours or 2 hours.

How many exams can I add?

Up to 3 upcoming exams on the free plan; unlimited with Premium. Past exams don’t count toward the limit.

What does a shared study recap include?

Your study time, number of completed sessions and streak for today or the last 7 days, with small StudyGlow branding. It never includes your name, subjects or exam names. An exam countdown (number of days only) appears only if you turn it on.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

StudyGlow has no cloud sync. Your data moves with your device when you set up a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. Premium can always be restored with Restore purchases.

How do I switch between light and dark appearance?

Go to Settings › Appearance and choose System, Dark or Light.
