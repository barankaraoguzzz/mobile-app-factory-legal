Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026 Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the StudyGlow iOS app (“StudyGlow”, “the app”) handles, where it goes and the choices you have. StudyGlow is published by Baran Karaoğuz, an individual developer based in Türkiye, under the studio brand Orvynel Labs (“we”, “us”). Orvynel Labs is a brand name, not a separate company. We are responsible for the processing described in this policy.

Summary

  • StudyGlow has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your study sessions, subjects, goals and exams are stored only on your iPhone.
  • If you allow it, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share Usage Analytics. Your subjects, exam names and anything else you type are never included.
  • Subscriptions are sold by Apple and managed with RevenueCat. We never receive your payment details.
  • StudyGlow does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, does not use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), shows no ads and does not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your iPhone

The app saves the following on your device so it works offline. We cannot access it.

WhatDetails
Study sessionsStart and end time, focused time, whether the session ended early, the calendar day, and the optional subject you type (for example “Organic Chemistry”).
A session in progressIts timing, length and optional subject, so the timer keeps running if the app is closed.
ExamsExam name, date and the optional color you choose.
SettingsDaily goal, last-used timer length, appearance, timer alerts, your usage analytics choice, and whether onboarding and a one-time Premium tip were shown.

Like other app data, this information may be included in iCloud or computer backups of your device, depending on your device settings. StudyGlow does not sync it to any other service.

2. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google, to learn which features are used and where the app can improve. It is used only while Share Usage Analytics is on.

Your choice

  • You can turn Share Usage Analytics on or off at any time in Settings › Privacy. When it is off, the app sends no analytics and Google Analytics collection, including automatic collection, is switched off.
  • On first launch the setting starts off if your device region is in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Türkiye, or cannot be determined, and on elsewhere. Your choice is saved and applies from then on.
  • Turning analytics off does not affect your subscription or any feature of the app.

What is collected when it is on

Events the app sends:

  • Onboarding completed, with the daily goal you chose.
  • Focus session started, paused, completed or ended early, with durations and minutes focused.
  • Exam created, with how many days away it is and its color category — never the exam name.
  • Study recap created, shared or saved, with the period, theme and whether an exam countdown is shown.
  • Premium screen viewed or closed, and subscription purchase or restore started, completed, cancelled, pending or failed, with the product identifier, where the action started and, for failures, a technical error description.

Information Google Analytics collects automatically:

  • An app instance identifier assigned to this installation of the app.
  • App activity such as first open, sessions, engagement time, screen views, app updates and operating system updates.
  • In-app purchase information processed by the App Store, such as product identifier, name and price.
  • Device and app information: device model and brand, operating system and version, app version and language setting.
  • General location (country, region, city) estimated from your IP address. The app does not use your device’s Location Services.

We do not set a user ID, and we do not link analytics to your name, email or Apple Account. Because the app does not use the advertising identifier, Google Analytics does not derive age, gender or interests, and advertising personalization signals are turned off. We keep user-level and event-level data in Google Analytics for 2 months; aggregated reports are not affected by that setting. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Subscriptions and payments

StudyGlow Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your payment card or billing details. See Apple’s privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate purchases and keep your Premium status current. RevenueCat processes this information on our behalf: your App Store transaction and receipt data (such as products, purchase and renewal dates, prices and renewal or refund status), when the app last checked your subscription, technical information such as device type and operating system, and an anonymous identifier generated by the app for your installation. We do not give RevenueCat your name, email or Apple Account. Subscription processing does not depend on your analytics choice.

4. Tracking and advertising

StudyGlow does not track you as defined by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency, does not access your advertising identifier and therefore does not ask for tracking permission. It contains no advertising and no advertising or attribution SDKs. If we add any feature that tracks, we will update this policy first, ask for your permission through Apple’s tracking prompt where required, and the app will keep working if you decline.

5. Permissions

Notifications

If you turn on timer alerts, StudyGlow asks for permission to show notifications. The alert is scheduled on your device for the end of your session. StudyGlow does not use push notifications, and no notification content is sent to us.

Photos

When you tap Save on a study recap, StudyGlow asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It cannot read your existing photos.

6. Study recaps you share

A study recap is an image created on your device from your study time, number of completed sessions and streak. It never includes your name, subjects or exam names. If you turn on “Show next exam countdown”, it shows only the number of days until your next exam. Sharing happens only when you choose to, through the iOS share sheet; the app or service you share to handles the image under its own policies.

7. Information from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with crash reports and diagnostics through App Store Connect. App Store sales reports we receive from Apple do not identify you.

9. International processing

We are based in Türkiye and our service providers operate internationally, so information they process may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where they or their sub-processors operate.

10. Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your iPhone: kept until you delete it. You can edit or delete exams in the app. Deleting StudyGlow from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device; copies in existing device backups remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.
  • Analytics: user-level and event-level data is kept for 2 months in Google Analytics. Turning Share Usage Analytics off stops further collection.
  • Subscription records: kept by RevenueCat on our behalf while needed to provide your subscription. Apple keeps its own purchase records under Apple’s policies.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. You can withdraw consent to analytics at any time with the Share Usage Analytics setting.

To make a request, email barankaraoguz.business@gmail.com. Because StudyGlow has no accounts, please include your App Store order ID for subscription requests. We may be unable to find analytics data, as it is not linked to your identity.

12. Children

StudyGlow is made for students, mainly young adults. It is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will address it.

13. Security

Your study data stays on your device, protected by iOS. Our service providers transmit data over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, give notice in the app or on this page before they take effect.
